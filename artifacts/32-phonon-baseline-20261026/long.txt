The morning train moved slowly through the valley. Outside the window, fields gave way to small towns and quiet roads. A passenger opened a notebook and began to describe the journey. She wrote about the changing light, the distant hills, and the people waiting on each platform. At the next stop, a family boarded with a picnic basket. They talked about the walk they planned to take beside the river. By noon the clouds had cleared, and sunlight filled the carriage. The train continued toward the coast, where the passengers would spend the afternoon exploring a new city.
